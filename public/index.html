About Leafcut

A small studio, built for clear, honest storytelling.

We help businesses turn ideas into clear, engaging videos that people understand, feel and respond to — not just watch. Our team has produced thousands of videos for startups, agencies and brands across SaaS, eCommerce and service businesses.

Every project runs through the same process: a clear brief, a tight script, considered visuals, and a final cut built for the platform it's meant to live on. No bloated timelines, no jargon — just video that does its job.
