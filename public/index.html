The Production Floor

Every video passes four sets of hands before it reaches you.

A real production line is not automation. It is division of labour — each person responsible for one thing, and good at it, instead of one editor doing everything and catching nothing.

Every project on our floor moves through a producer who owns the brief, an editor who builds the cut, a quality lead who checks it against your brand system frame by frame, and a creative lead who signs it off before it ships. Four people, four checkpoints, on a 90-second video.

That is why the fortieth video looks like the first one. Video programmes rarely fail on video one — they fail on video forty, when the producer has picked up three other accounts and the newest editor has never read your brand guide. A line does not have that failure mode. A freelancer does.

    FAQ

    Questions worth asking before you commit

    How fast can you deliver?

    You choose: 24, 48 or 72 hours, or five business days as standard. You set the clock at the point of brief and we quote against it.

    Can you handle forty videos a month?

    Yes, and considerably more. Our systems were built to run 500 to 1,000 finished videos a month. Monthly capacity is agreed in writing before we start.

    What if our volume spikes?

    That is what the headroom is for. Tell us on the call and we will tell you honestly whether the spike is absorbable. We would rather decline a month than miss it.

    Do you shoot on location?

    No, and it is deliberate. We produce live-action work from premium licensed footage and AI-generated sequences, directed and cut like original production. No crew, no scheduling, no reshoot when the script changes.

    Do you use AI?

    Yes, openly, and it is why our economics work. Generative video, synthetic presenters, voice synthesis and AI localisation, all directed by senior people with a human final cut. Full written disclosure available for your compliance team.

    What happens to our confidential material?

    It stays yours. Your material is never used to train third-party models. NDA signed before any brief is shared, as standard.

    Where are you based?

    Kathmandu, Nepal. One floor, one team, covering every time zone, every day of the year.

    What is the minimum engagement?

    A paid pilot: one video, fixed price, credited in full against your first month if you continue.

    Who will we actually work with?

    A named producer who owns your account and does not rotate, plus a creative lead who signs off every cut. You will meet both before you commit.
