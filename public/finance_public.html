The Retention Reel

A twelve-month cohort analysis of Biiyond's Production board — who ordered, who came back, and what it was worth.

Window Aug 2025 – Jul 2026 Source ClickUp workspace export, Aug 22 2026 Tasks analyzed 2,188 Clients 1,404
01 — Volume

New orders keep climbing. Repeat orders don't.

Task volume by month, split by whether the client had ordered before that month. Returning-client volume has stayed roughly flat around 60/month even as new intake has grown — the growth line is almost entirely new logos, not existing clients ordering more.

New clients Returning clients
Returning clients made up 31% of client-months in this window — meaning the other 69% came from someone ordering for the first time.
02 — Retention

Most clients place one order and never come back

Each row is a monthly cohort — the clients who placed their first-ever order that month by order date (measured against the full order history back to Apr 2025, not just this window). Each cell is the share of that cohort still ordering in a later month.

Retention 0% ~8% 15%+
Average month-2 retention across all cohorts is ~9%. The Nov 2025 and Dec 2025 cohorts held up best (~14% into month 2); Feb 2026 and May 2026 have the weakest month-2 return rate.
03 — Revenue

Revenue is up ~1.7× since August, margin held at ~59%

Net price billed and contribution margin per month, attributed by order date (Date Created) — every month shown here is a fully closed calendar month as of this export, so none of these figures will keep growing after the fact. Margin has stayed remarkably stable through the growth. Revenue peaks in Jun 2026 and holds roughly flat into Jul.

The reason the margin has not changed significantly is that it is calculated relative to the net price. For projects completed in-house, we have estimated a flat cost rate of 50% per project, so the contribution margin percentage remains relatively stable as revenue grows. The 9–12% variation in margin is primarily driven by freelancer projects, where we have the actual project cost rather than an estimated flat-rate cost. As the mix of freelancer and in-house projects changes from month to month, this creates some variation in the overall contribution margin.

Net price Contribution margin
12-month total, by order date: $228.2K net price billed, $134.7K contribution margin (59.0% average margin). Jun 2026 is the peak month at $26.7K, with Jul 2026 close behind at $25.0K — both fully closed months by order date. See "Why order date, not List" below for why this report doesn't use ClickUp's own month-list totals.

Methodology & notes

Cohort month. A client's cohort is the calendar month of their first-ever task by order date, across the full history available (Apr 2025 onward — May 2025 is excluded, see below) — so a client who first ordered in Apr 2025 and reappears in Sep 2025 is correctly counted as "returning," not "new." But the reapeat client before who have multiple orders before Apr 2025 and reappears in this time range is counted as a new client
