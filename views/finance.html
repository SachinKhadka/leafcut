The Retention Reel

A twelve-month cohort analysis of Biiyond's Production board — who ordered, who came back, and what it was worth.

Window Aug 2025 – Jul 2026 Source ClickUp workspace export, Aug 22 2026 Tasks analyzed 2,317 Clients 1,500
01 — Volume

New orders keep climbing. Repeat orders don't.

Task volume by month, split by whether the client had ordered before that month. Returning-client volume has stayed roughly flat around 60/month even as new intake has grown — the growth line is almost entirely new logos, not existing clients ordering more.

02 — Retention

Most clients place one order and never come back

Each row is a monthly cohort — the clients who placed their first-ever order that month (measured against the full order history back to Mar 2025, not just this window). Each cell is the share of that cohort still ordering in a later month.

Average month-2 retention across all cohorts is ~9%. The Nov 2025 and Dec 2025 cohorts held up best (14–15% into month 2); Oct 2025 and Aug 2025 have the weakest month-2 return rate.

Revenue is up 3.5× since August, margin held at ~59%

Net price billed and contribution margin per month (figures as recorded in ClickUp's pricing fields). Margin has stayed remarkably stable through the growth — the July spike is volume-driven, not a pricing or cost shift.

Methodology & notes

Scope. Every task in Space "Client's Project" → Folder "Production", drawn from a full-history ClickUp export (Mar 2025–Jul 2026, 3,131 tasks) provided directly by the user on Aug 22, 2026. The report windows to the 12 most recent complete-to-partial months (Aug 2025–Jul 2026, 2,317 tasks) for volume, revenue and quality metrics.

Cohort month. A client's cohort is the calendar month of their first-ever task across the full history we have (Mar 2025–Jul 2026), not just this window — so a client who first ordered in Apr 2025 and reappears in Sep 2025 is correctly counted as "returning," not "new."

Client identity. Parsed from the "Ordered by" / "Client name" custom fields, falling back to the task-name prefix before the parenthesis (e.g. "abe12345 (LaunchpadTV)" → abe12345) when a field was blank. Minor spelling/casing variants of the same handle were merged. Tasks previously logged under the placeholder identity "unknown" were identified as belonging to a real client and are now included under the name Nacho.

Exclusions & caveats

  • 4 tasks tagged to the placeholder identity "biiyond" (internal projects) were excluded from client-level counts — they aren't a real client account.
  • Financial figures reflect the "Total price," "Net Price," and "Contribution Margin" fields as entered in ClickUp; currency/units weren't independently verified against billing records.
  • The "Rating" field is heavily ceiling'd — 95% of rated tasks show a perfect 5.0 — so it's reported for completeness but isn't a strong differentiator between months.
  • Cancellations are inferred from a "canceled" tag rather than a status, since cancelled ClickUp tasks in this workspace are still marked "completed."
  • Data limitation — repeat-client scope. Order and revision/reorder history is only recorded from March 2025 onward; nothing earlier was exported from ClickUp. A client who was ordering from Leafcut before March 2025 but placed only one order between March 2025 and July 2026 is classified here as a one-time / new client — their earlier orders simply aren't in the data available. Treat "new" client counts as an upper bound, not a certainty.
